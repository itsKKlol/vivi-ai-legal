User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Users of ViVi AI by Verve by Khazri may request deletion of eligible personal information associated with their use of ViVi AI.

How to Request Data Deletion

  1. Contact Verve by Khazri through the official contact channel associated with ViVi AI.
  2. Clearly state that you are requesting "ViVi AI Data Deletion".
  3. Provide sufficient information to identify the WhatsApp account or interaction associated with the request.
  4. We may request reasonable verification to confirm that the requester is authorized to request deletion of the relevant data.

What Happens After a Request

After a valid request has been verified, eligible information controlled by ViVi AI and associated with the request will be reviewed for deletion or anonymization.

Certain information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, transaction records, dispute resolution, legal obligations, or other legitimate operational requirements.

Third-Party Data

These instructions apply to eligible information controlled by ViVi AI. Information independently processed or retained by third-party services such as WhatsApp or Meta is subject to the respective provider's privacy and deletion processes.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how ViVi AI handles information, please read our Privacy Policy.

Deletion request: Please use the official contact information associated with ViVi AI / Verve by Khazri and include the words "ViVi AI Data Deletion" in your request.
