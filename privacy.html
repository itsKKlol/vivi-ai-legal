Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ViVi AI by Verve by Khazri handles information when users communicate with ViVi AI through WhatsApp and related Verve services.

1. Information We Process

When you interact with ViVi AI, we may process information necessary to provide the service. This may include your WhatsApp phone number or identifier, profile or display name when supplied by WhatsApp, messages you send, menu selections, order-related information, and other information you voluntarily provide.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to messages, provide customer assistance, display menu information, provide recommendations, maintain relevant conversation context, process customer requests, support Verve services, maintain service reliability, and prevent misuse or abuse.

3. WhatsApp and Meta

ViVi AI uses the WhatsApp Business Platform. Information transmitted through WhatsApp may also be processed by Meta according to Meta's applicable privacy policies and terms.

4. Artificial Intelligence Processing

Some messages may be processed using artificial intelligence services to generate conversational responses and provide requested functionality.

5. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, provide customer support, maintain operational records, protect the service, resolve issues, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and operational safeguards are used to protect information handled by the service. However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of information associated with their use of ViVi AI.

See our User Data Deletion Instructions for information about submitting a deletion request.

8. Third-Party Services

ViVi AI may rely on third-party services, including WhatsApp, Meta services, hosting infrastructure, and artificial intelligence providers. These services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ViVi AI features, integrations, or operational requirements change.

10. Contact

Privacy questions and data-related requests may be submitted through the official contact information associated with ViVi AI and Verve by Khazri.
